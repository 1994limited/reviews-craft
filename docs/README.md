# Reviews documentation

Reviews collects reviews and star ratings on your own Craft site: of entries, Commerce products, or both. Editors moderate and reply in the control panel; your templates show them with a ready-made section or your own markup; search engines get review schema.

## Setting up

1. [Installation](installation.md): requirements, installing, the first settings.
2. [Ready-made reviews](ready-made.md): one tag for the average, the reviews and the form, with styles that match your site.
3. [Moderation](moderation.md): the reviews screen, replies, permissions, emails, the dashboard widget and the Reviews field.

## On your site

4. [Templates](templates.md): `craft.reviews`, review queries, and writing your own form.
5. [Photos](photos.md): reviewers' photos, kept off the site until a review is approved.
6. [Spam](spam.md): the hidden checks, rate limits and captchas.
7. [Search engines](seo.md): review schema, by itself or in SEOmatic's.
8. [Caching](caching.md): Blitz, `{% cache %}` and statically cached pages.
